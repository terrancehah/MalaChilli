# Design Context

## Users

**Primary Audience:** Malaysian diners (multi-racial: Malay, Chinese, Indian, mixed communities) who visit local restaurants (kopitiam, mamak, cafes).

**Context:** Mobile-first usage - scanning QR codes at restaurant counters, sharing referral codes via WhatsApp/Facebook, checking virtual currency balances on-the-go.

**Jobs to be Done:**
- Get guaranteed 5% discounts on first visits
- Earn passive income (1% of downline spending) through referrals
- Track VC earnings and redemptions across multiple restaurants
- Share promotion codes easily with friends and family

**User Emotions:** Excited about earning rewards, confident in the platform's security, delighted by the viral sharing mechanics, motivated to grow their referral network.

## Brand Personality

**Voice:** Friendly, encouraging, local

MakanTak speaks like a helpful friend who knows the local food scene - warm, approachable, and straightforward. Think "friendly kopitiam uncle, not corporate bank."

**Tone Characteristics:**
- **Friendly:** Warm and approachable, like a friend recommending a restaurant
- **Encouraging:** Motivates users to take action without being pushy
- **Clear:** Simple language that anyone can understand
- **Helpful:** Guides users through problems with solutions
- **Local:** Feels Malaysian - uses familiar terms (RM, kopitiam) and cultural context

## Aesthetic Direction

**Visual Tone:** Modern, warm, and inviting with a Malaysian local flavor

**Current Theme:** Warm orange & beige palette with glassmorphism effects
- Primary: Vibrant Orange (#FF6D1F) - energetic, food-related
- Secondary: Beige highlights (#F5E7C6) - warm, approachable
- Background: Warm cream (#FFF8E1) - soft, inviting

**Typography:**
- Headings: Quicksand (friendly, rounded)
- Body: Montserrat (clean, readable)
- Decorative: Pacifico (playful accent)

**Visual Style:**
- Glass morphism for cards and modals (modern, premium feel)
- Warm gradients (orange to amber)
- Rounded corners (friendly, approachable)
- Mobile-optimized with swipeable bottom sheets

**Theme Preference:** Light mode primary (food/dining context suggests daytime browsing, coffee break usage)

**Anti-References:**
- Corporate banking apps (too formal, cold)
- Generic admin dashboards (too technical, boring)
- Overly gamified apps (too childish, distracting)

## Design Principles

1. **Mobile-First & Touch-Friendly**
   - Minimum 44×44px touch targets (prefer 48×48px)
   - Swipeable bottom sheets for mobile interactions
   - Responsive typography scaling (text-heavy pages: 16-18px body, interaction-heavy: 14-16px)

2. **Friendly & Encouraging**
   - Warm color palette (orange, beige, cream)
   - Celebratory success states ("Transaction successful! 🎉")
   - Helpful, solution-oriented error messages
   - Empty states that guide users to take action

3. **Local & Inclusive**
   - Multi-language support (English, Bahasa Melayu, Simplified Chinese)
   - Malaysian cultural sensitivity (halal-friendly, dietary inclusivity)
   - Local terminology (RM, kopitiam, mamak references)

4. **Clear Visual Hierarchy**
   - Consistent 4px spacing scale
   - Semantic color usage (success, warning, error, info)
   - Modular type scale with clear contrast (1.25+ ratio between steps)
   - Glass morphism for elevation and depth

5. **Accessible & Inclusive**
   - WCAG AA contrast ratios (with industry exceptions for white-on-orange for large text)
   - Reduced motion support (@media prefers-reduced-motion)
   - Semantic HTML and proper ARIA labels
   - Focus states for keyboard navigation

## Key Differentiators

**What makes MakanTak unforgettable:**
- Viral referral mechanics (3-level upline rewards) - unique to Malaysian F&B
- Restaurant-specific virtual currency (not generic points)
- Warm, local aesthetic (not corporate, not generic startup)
- Mobile-first with native-feeling interactions (swipeable sheets, QR scanning)
